About us

## Where anime dreams meet specialty coffee

MEOW MEOW Café is a Japanese-inspired café that blends modern luxury with the colours and wonder of anime culture. Every drink is crafted with premium beans, real fruit and a little theatre, and every corner is made for photos, friends and long afternoons.

Our fictional brand ambassador, **Gojo Satoru** from *Jujutsu Kaisen*, inspires the blue-and-white palette and the playful “limitless” spirit of the menu. This is a fan-inspired concept; Gojo Satoru is a character owned by his creators and is not affiliated with this café.
